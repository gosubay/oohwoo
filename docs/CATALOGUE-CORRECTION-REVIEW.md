## How to listen

1. Start the game server (`start-game.bat`).
2. Open <http://localhost:8080/tools/catalogue-listening.html>. Pick a song, press a section: it plays that part of the recording (or the singer only) and shows the charted notes and words.
3. In the game, play the song with "Hear my guide melody" ticked, in Full song and in Quick play.
4. Tell Claude which sections are good and what is off. Approval is written into `tools/chart-builder/songs/<key>.json`; nothing else can mark a section verified.
