## Permission and conversion

[IPOS guidance](https://ask.gov.sg/ipos/questions/clglxzrrv00f5l408in5a0ozr)
states that using a protected work generally requires permission or a licence.
Generating a fresh piano sound avoids using an existing sound recording; it does
not remove copyright in the composition or a protected arrangement.

Before publishing a level, record the exact work/version, permission holder,
permitted game/app uses and territories, and score source/arrangement permission.
On 11 October 2026, Galvin confirmed there is no existing app-use permission and
chose to keep these songs listed until licensed. Keep the eight CNY entries
unavailable; do not add their melodies or publish playable levels yet.

Once suitable source material is available, extract the vocal melody (not all
right-hand piano notes), preserve rests and tied notes, expand repeats, and verify
pitch and duration against the score. MusicXML or MIDI is preferable; scanned
scores require transcription and visual checks. Use the same note events for the
game targets and generated audio, as the Christmas practice levels do.
