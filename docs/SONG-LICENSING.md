# Song licensing — what we can add (stated 2026-10-03, Galvin)

Not legal advice. Get a lawyer's view before selling the game or listing it in an app store.

## How a song gets cleared

A song has two copyrights, and we need both:

1. **Composition** (melody + lyrics). It must be **public domain**: the composer and lyricist died
   70+ years ago, or it's traditional/anonymous. (In the US, anything published before 1931 is also
   public domain.) Use the old traditional words, never a modern arrangement's added lines.
2. **Recording**. We make our own: Galvin sings, and Suno clones the voice into the backing MP3. All
   current backings were made this way (Galvin, 2026-10-03).
   - Suno only grants commercial rights for tracks made on a paid plan. Confirm the plan for each
     generation.

Our own recording only fixes #2. A modern composition is still not ours, even when Galvin sings it.

## Adding a song — pipeline (unchanged)

**Christmas practice exception, 2026-10-10:** Galvin chose score-based melody practice with
locally generated piano-style audio for six Christmas carols. This is a separate practice
route; see `docs/CAROL-PRACTICE.md` for checked-in score sources and conversion details.
The original recording catalogue continues to follow the pipeline below.

The game needs an MP3 for every song. Players hear it, and every pipe is timed to a timestamp in it.
Notes still come from that recording (see "Song charts" in CLAUDE.md). A public-domain score
(MusicXML/MIDI/ABC from Mutopia, IMSLP, abcnotation.com) is only a **reference** to flag wrong pitches,
never a replacement for the recording's timing. Suno often changes rhythm and melody slightly, and the
chart must match what the player hears.
